Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-02. Virtual Try-On processes account, catalog, and photos to provide virtual try-on and commerce features.

Data and purposes

  • Account identifiers and authentication data: sign-in, security, and account support.
  • Person and garment photos: try-on processing and saved wardrobe/catalog features, only after the requested action.
  • Purchase tokens and product IDs: server-side Google Play purchase verification and entitlement fulfilment. We do not put service-account credentials in the app.
  • Diagnostics and abuse-prevention signals: reliability, rate limiting, and fraud prevention.

Adults-only image policy

The AI Try-On service is intended only for users aged 18 or older. Before cloud processing, users must confirm that they are at least 18 and will upload only their own image or an image they are authorized to use. Images of minors and sensitive images are prohibited. We use content-safety and single-person checks to reduce misuse, but we do not claim to verify identity or exact age from a face and we do not collect identity documents for age verification.

Processors and retention

Supabase provides authentication, database, and controlled API storage. Modal runs requested inference jobs. Cloudflare R2 stores media objects under access-controlled keys. Google Play processes purchases. Android SDKs (including MediaPipe and ONNX Runtime) process applicable images on-device.

  • Completed AI Try-On job records and stored result objects are scheduled for deletion after 30 days. User-requested deletion may remove them sooner where legally possible.
  • AI result reports are retained for up to 24 months for safety, abuse review, and dispute handling.
  • Commerce idempotency records are retained for 7 days and rate-limit state for 2 days.
  • Purchase, entitlement, refund, fraud-prevention, and transaction records are retained only for applicable legal, accounting, refund, dispute, and security obligations, with access restricted.
  • Account and profile data remain while the account is active and are deleted or irreversibly de-identified after a verified deletion request, except for the restricted records described above. Backups expire through their normal rotation.

Sharing and rights

We do not sell personal data. We disclose data only to the processors above, service providers needed for the requested feature, or when legally required. You may access, correct, export, or delete your account and request deletion of associated personal data. Catalog imagery must have documented commercial rights; see the release catalog-rights manifest.

Affiliate disclosure

Some catalog or merchant links may be affiliate links. If you buy through one, we may receive a commission at no extra cost to you. Affiliate status never changes ranking, eligibility, or price.

Contact and deletion

For privacy requests, use the account deletion page or contact the publisher through the support address shown in the Google Play listing. Requests are verified to protect the account owner.
