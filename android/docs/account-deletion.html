Account and data deletion

To request deletion, open Virtual Try-On → Profile → Account → Delete account. The signed-in request enters a 24-hour safety window before processing. If you cannot sign in, contact the publisher through the support address shown on the Google Play listing and include the account email plus “Account deletion request”. Do not send passwords or purchase tokens.

What is deleted

After identity verification, we delete or irreversibly de-identify the account profile, customer-uploaded photos, wardrobe items, saved projects, and non-required diagnostics from Supabase and associated R2 storage. Completed AI Try-On jobs and stored result objects are otherwise scheduled for deletion after 30 days.

What may be retained

Purchase, entitlement, refund, fraud-prevention, and transaction records may be retained only for applicable legal, accounting, dispute, and security obligations. They are access-restricted and then deleted or anonymized. AI result reports may be retained for up to 24 months for safety and dispute handling. Backups expire through their normal rotation. We will confirm receipt and completion or explain any lawful exception.

Deleting the app does not delete the account. Account deletion does not cancel an active Google Play subscription; cancel it separately in Google Play.
